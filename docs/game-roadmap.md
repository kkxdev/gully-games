# Game roadmap

## MVP

1. **Pen Fight** — school desk; playable physics, touch, CPU, first-to-three matches.
2. **Kanche** — gully; marble flicking and circle rules. Validate shared input needs before extracting helpers.
3. **Book Cricket** — school desk; page scoring and innings.
4. **Hand Cricket** — courtyard; number selection and CPU innings.

## Next

5. **Gilli-Danda** — gully; strike timing and distance.
6. **Pithoo** — playground; stack, throw and rebuild.
7. **Kite Fighting** — rooftop; wind and line control.

## Future

Gully Cricket, Lattoo, Stapoo, Rumal Jhappatta, Chain-Chain, Vish-Amrit, Chor-Police, Name Place Animal Thing, Paper Plane and Tyre Race. Kho-Kho and Kabaddi also belong in the eventual playground.

Develop location navigation after a few validated games. Progression and social systems follow proven player needs. Introduce a backend only for concrete requirements such as shared leaderboards or authoritative multiplayer.
