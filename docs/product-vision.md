# Product vision

Gully Games recreates Indian childhood play from the 1980s and 1990s. A coherent neighbourhood becomes the lobby: school desks host Pen Fight, Book Cricket and Name Place Animal Thing; the gully hosts Kanche, Gully Cricket and Gilli-Danda; the playground hosts Pithoo, Kho-Kho, Kabaddi and Chain-Chain; rooftops host Kite Fighting.

The first milestone establishes a quality playable desk game and responsive web/PWA platform. Wooden grain, original stationery, notebook lines and warm paper evoke school life without copyrighted brands. Touch is a primary interaction.

Avatars, progression, collectibles, achievements, tournaments, leaderboards, multiplayer, regional rules and richer environments are future possibilities. Build them only around demonstrated needs. No backend is needed now.
